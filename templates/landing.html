The problem

One missing comma and Google stops reading your markup.

Writing JSON-LD by hand means knowing Schema.org's vocabulary, which properties are required, and which data types Google's rich results actually accept.

A typo in a property name or a missing field is invisible until a rich result silently fails to appear.
